Thousands of parts. Many look the same.

Bushes, rivets and pins that differ by a few millimetres sit in the same racks. Telling them apart takes years at the counter.

The knowledge lived in people's heads.

What a part is, where it sits and what it costs was remembered, not written down. A new staff member had to ask every time.

So we made the part findable.

Every part gets a photo, a rack, a code and its dimensions, so the right one can be picked out of the crowd.

One photo, one answer.

The counter no longer depends on one person's memory. Anyone can identify the part and bill it.

The hard part was never the AI.

For a model to recognise a part, there has to be a correct catalogue to match against. That catalogue did not exist.

3,000 parts, recorded by hand.

One at a time, on the warehouse floor. It was slow, and I enjoyed it so much I did not notice when the toughest part was behind me.

Then it became a system.

Staff record rack, photo, name and stock; the owner adds price and supplier. The catalogue now grows without one person doing every entry.

5,580 parts today, still growing.

And every part is now being measured, which is the data the next identification step needs.

Take a photo of the part.

Hold it flat, label side up. The phone does the rest; there is nothing to type.

Claude Vision reads it.

It looks at shape, finish and markings, and compares them with the photos and details in the catalogue.

You get the match, ready to bill.

Best match first with stock, price and rack, plus similar parts in case it is a near miss. One tap adds it to the bill.

Next: dimensions settle it.

A Roboflow model will add length and diameter matching, so near-identical parts are told apart for certain. It switches on once every part is measured.

It runs the whole business, not just the counter.

Identification is the front door. Everything a spare parts business does every day sits behind it, on the same phone.

Billing

Counter and wholesale bills with cash, UPI or credit.

Stock and racks

Live stock for 5,580 parts, with rack locations and low-stock alerts.

Collections

Who owes what, and a WhatsApp reminder in one tap.

Suppliers and orders

Purchase orders, deliveries back into stock, and what is due to pay.

Works offline

Sales save on the phone and sync the moment the connection returns.

Staff and roles

Every person logs in with their own PIN; admin, staff and accounts see what they need.

Taken to production at the AI hackathon.

In one sprint, a single 1.4 MB HTML file became a real deployment, and the live database moved from Firebase to Convex without stopping the business. Every record was checked before the switch.
